# PortfolioPath v25 UX fixes

- Comparison is intended to open once 2–3 investments are selected.
- Removed post-selection "why did you choose this?" research checkpoint questions.
- Category progression is no longer blocked by a research checkpoint.
- A user can continue once at least one holding is selected in the category.
- Research links and comparison tools remain available, but research is optional rather than a gating task.
