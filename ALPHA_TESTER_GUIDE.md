# PortfolioPath Alpha Tester Guide

Thank you for testing PortfolioPath.

PortfolioPath is an educational portfolio-construction and research tool. The alpha is focused on whether the workflow is understandable and whether the calculations behave as expected.

## Suggested test
Create a practice portfolio using an amount you find realistic.

Work through:
- investor profile
- recommended allocation
- guided builder
- stock/ETF research
- simulated trades
- performance
- portfolio review
- insights
- alerts

## What to report
Please note:
- anything confusing
- calculations that look wrong
- screens that feel too technical
- screens that feel too simplistic
- broken links or buttons
- slow pages
- mobile layout problems
- investment explanations you do not understand

## Important
Do not use the alpha as the sole basis for real-money investing. Market data and calculations can be incomplete or delayed during testing.
