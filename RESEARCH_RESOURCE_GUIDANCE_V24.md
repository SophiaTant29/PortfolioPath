# PortfolioPath v24 — Research Resource Guidance

Every investment card now includes a visible research-resource panel.

## Stocks
- Investor Relations
- SEC Filings
- Yahoo Finance
- Morningstar

## ETFs
- Official Fund Page
- SEC Prospectus & Filings
- Yahoo Finance
- Morningstar

For every link, PortfolioPath explains:
1. Why the source is useful
2. What the user should look for there

This keeps the product educational rather than just sending beginners to external websites without context.
